## Form Coverage

The admin form mockup is designed to capture:

- Incident classification: safeguarding concern, accident / first aid, behaviour incident, bullying / peer-on-peer abuse, online safety, medical / medication, physical intervention, near miss, and off-site trip.
- Severity and confidentiality: Low, Medium, High, Critical; Staff only, Head/DSL, or Parent viewable after sign-off.
- When and where: date, time, location, activity/context, and whether the incident happened on a trip.
- People involved: child or children, staff, witnesses, and recorder.
- Factual account: what happened, direct words / disclosure, and immediate actions taken.
- Injury and medical details: injury sustained, body area, first aid given, first aider, emergency services / NHS contact, hospital treatment, and parent/carer notification.
- Reportability checks: DSL, Head sign-off, Pastor/Principal escalation, LADO consideration for staff/adult allegations, children's social care / police referral, HSE/RIDDOR check, and recorded reason for parent sharing.
- Parent PDF controls: parent summary, redactions, attachments, preview, download, and sharing after sign-off.

## Parent Visibility Rule

Parent visibility should default to off. A parent-viewable PDF should only be generated after approval, with a recorded sharing reason and any redactions applied. The parent page should show signed-off, parent-safe report summaries and PDF actions, not internal safeguarding notes.
